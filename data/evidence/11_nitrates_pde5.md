# Nitrates and PDE5 inhibitors

Summary written for the PolySafe prototype. Sources: FDA prescribing information for sildenafil (Viagra, Revatio), Contraindications section; FDA prescribing information for isosorbide mononitrate and nitroglycerin.

## Sildenafil + nitrates (contraindicated)
Nitrates (isosorbide mononitrate, nitroglycerin/glyceryl trinitrate) release nitric oxide, which increases cGMP; sildenafil blocks the enzyme (PDE5) that breaks cGMP down. Together they cause additive vasodilation and can produce severe, potentially fatal hypotension. The sildenafil label contraindicates use with nitrates in any form, whether taken regularly or intermittently. This combination should be escalated for prescriber review immediately.

## Two nitrates
A long-acting nitrate (isosorbide mononitrate) with as-needed sublingual nitroglycerin for angina is a common intended regimen. Two regular long-acting nitrates are rarely needed and should be confirmed.
